STRATEGY ACROSS SIX AGES

Age of Realms

Grow a medieval settlement into a modern and futuristic realm. Outlast your rivals in a world where people age, veterans earn their rank, rivers must be bridged and water must be clean.

Play
  • Seljuk and Ottoman warriors, villagers and cavalry
  • Modern rifles and tanks; future plasma weapons and walkers
  • Mortality, elder monks and veteran ranks
  • Rivers, lakes and seas with living fish stocks
  • Wells and clean water for your people
  • Canals, trenches, planted forests, bridges and tunnels
Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended. Built on the Special Build of the Opus 5.5 effort-comparison experiment — the original builds are kept in legacy/.
