Three real-world battlefields

North-up tactical grids made from public-domain Natural Earth coastlines and lakes. Hills, forests, resources and starting positions are arranged for play. Choose these maps in Game Setup or the Map Editor; Large is recommended for the World map.

Blue: water · Green: land · Brown: mountains · Gold: example player starts. The World extent is 60°S–85°N, covering the inhabited continents. Made with Natural Earth.
